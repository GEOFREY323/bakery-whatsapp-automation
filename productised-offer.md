Template 1 — Discovery Question List (drafted)
The business:

What do you sell? To whom?

How many orders per day/week?

What is your average order value?

The process (walk through yesterday's real orders):

Walk me through yesterday's orders step by step.

"And then what happens?" — until the end state.

"What happens when that goes wrong?" — at every step.

The pain (quantified):

How many hours per week does this take?

How much money is lost from missed or wrong orders?

What are your exact words for this problem?

Constraints:

What tools do you already use?

Who else must approve changes?

What must never change? (e.g., "customers must always reach a human")

Dependencies:

Do you have WhatsApp Business API access?

Do you have a Google account for Sheets?

Are you the sole decision-maker?

Why Productised Beats Hourly

Fixed pricing flips it. Speed becomes pure margin.

Niching compounds. Every food-business client teaches you the vertical's patterns, making you faster AND more credible with the next one.
